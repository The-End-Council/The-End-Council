<summary>More About Me</summary>

I am a system engineer working as a full-stack developer.

I mainly work with web systems, backend development, frontend development, databases, automation, and content-driven projects.

Outside of engineering work, I operate a blog called **The End Council**, where I write about programming, AI, advanced technology, investment, productivity, and survival strategies for the chaotic modern era.

The End Council is built around a dark sci-fi world where characters such as Chaos, Abyss, and Null discuss technology, AI, programming, investment, and life strategy.

This profile is not only a portfolio.
It is a record of building, learning, writing, and surviving in the AI era.
